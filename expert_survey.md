# Expert Evaluation Survey — Endodontic AI Validation

Use a 5-point Likert scale unless stated otherwise: 1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree.

1. The application's diagnostic output is clinically plausible.
2. The application identifies clinically important findings from the checklist.
3. The proposed management pathway is clinically useful as decision support.
4. The output is clear enough to support clinical discussion.
5. The application could be useful as an adjunct in endodontic assessment.
