# Model Card — Endodontic Synthetic V2

## Intended purpose
Research prototype for testing whether a classifier trained exclusively on synthetic tabular endodontic cases can generalize to real, previously unseen patient cases.

## Training data
- Synthetic only.
- Generated by `generate_synthetic_v2.py`.
- Default build: 20,000 cases.
- The generator uses pre-specified probabilistic relationships between diagnostic labels and clinical findings.
- These probabilities are research assumptions and must undergo independent expert review before the prospective validation model is formally frozen.

## Outputs
Two separate multiclass predictions:
1. Pulpal diagnosis.
2. Apical diagnosis.

The management pathway is generated transparently from predicted diagnoses and selected management modifiers; it is not an autonomous prescription.

## Important limitations
- Synthetic hold-out performance is not evidence of real-patient clinical performance.
- The generator may encode expert assumptions and may omit real-world complexity.
- Model probabilities are not clinically calibrated until evaluated on real patients.
- Current V2 excludes previously treated/initiated teeth, primary teeth, and several special endodontic scenarios.
- Reference-standard experts must remain blinded to AI predictions during external validation.

## Model-freeze rule
After the final prospective validation model is selected, record its model file hash, dataset hash, generator version, code commit, and freeze date. No training, tuning, recalibration or threshold modification may use any case from the primary real-patient validation cohort.
